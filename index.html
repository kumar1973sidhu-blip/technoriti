FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about staying at BCE Bhagalpur

How do I register for accommodation? +
Click the "BOOK NOW" button or open the Dedicated Accommodation Form. Enter your details, choose your days and hostel preference, and confirm your booking.
What is the fee breakdown for staying? +
Lodging is ₹50 per person per day. Food (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) is ₹125 per person per day. The total all-inclusive fee is ₹175 per person per day.
Can we book as a group or team? +
Yes! Teams and contingents can book together. Groups with over 4 participants receive flat ₹150 off, and groups of 12+ receive flat ₹250 off.
What documents are mandatory for room allotment? +
1) College ID Card (2 photocopies + original), 2) Aadhaar / PAN or Government ID (2 copies), 3) 3 passport size photos, 4) College NOC, and 5) Copy of registration/payment slip.
What amenities and facilities are provided? +
High-speed campus Wi-Fi, 24/7 security guard presence, clean RO drinking water, power points for device charging, recreation/common room, and emergency first-aid/medical aid.
Can faculty mentors or parents stay? +
Yes, faculty coordinators and accompanying mentors can avail guest accommodation upon prior notification to the hostel logistics desk (Prof. Kumar Jyotiraditya: 7004618331).
