Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about staying at BCE Bhagalpur

How do I register for accommodation? +
You can book your stay by clicking the "BOOK NOW" button or visiting the Register section on this portal. Check the "Need Campus Accommodation" option and submit your details.
Can we book as a group? +
Yes! Teams and college contingents can book together. Groups with over 4 participants receive group tariff discounts.
What documents are mandatory for room allotment? +
1) College ID Card (2 photocopies + original), 2) Aadhaar / PAN or Government ID (2 copies), 3) 3 passport size photos, 4) College NOC, and 5) Copy of payment slip.
Does the accommodation fee include food? +
Lodging is ₹50 per person per day. Mess dining (hygienic breakfast, lunch, and dinner) is ₹125 per day served in the college dining hall.
Will team members be given accommodation together? +
Yes, participants from the same team or institution of the same gender are allotted shared rooms together based on availability.
Can we purchase accommodation for parents or faculty mentors? +
Yes, faculty coordinators and accompanying mentors can avail guest accommodation upon prior notification to the hostel logistics desk.
